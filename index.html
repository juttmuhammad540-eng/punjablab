Before your test.

General guidance. Your doctor will explain your results.

Do I need to fast?

Fast for 8 to 10 hours (water is fine) before fasting sugar and lipid profile. CBC, thyroid, HbA1c and vitamin D usually need no fasting.

What does a CBC show?

It counts red cells, white cells and platelets, helping doctors look for anaemia, infection and clotting problems.

What is HbA1c?

It reflects your average blood sugar over 2 to 3 months and helps monitor diabetes. No fasting is needed.

When is an X-ray needed?

Usually when a doctor suspects a fracture, a lung problem or a joint injury. Bring your doctor’s slip if you have one.
